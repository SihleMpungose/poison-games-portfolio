LATEST DEVLOG

What changed.

Posts are chronological and intentionally narrower than the main portfolio sections.

18 OCT 2026PRIORITIESPOISON GAMES

Current focus: support first, expansion later.

I am narrowing my active workload. Mobile MMA Sim is now in post-launch support, and Unwritten Meridian remains the other main priority while it is in Early Access. TrackBorn and Online MMA Sim are paused so I can give released and player-facing work proper attention instead of splitting development across too many games.

What this changes: fewer parallel projects, more time for testing, fixes, release quality and player support.
13 OCT 2026FULL RELEASEMOBILE MMA SIM

Mobile MMA Sim reached full Google Play release.

Version 3.0.0.0 marked the move from Early Access into full release on Google Play. The important lesson now is that launch is not the finish line: support, regression testing and careful fixes become part of the product after players are already inside it.

Google Play ↗ itch.io full edition ↗
18 OCT 2026EARLY ACCESSUNWRITTEN MERIDIAN

Unwritten Meridian remains in Early Access on both platforms.

Book One is currently available in Early Access on itch.io and Google Play. My focus is on supporting the current build and preparing it carefully for the next release stage rather than publishing a rushed date update.

View on itch.io ↗
18 OCT 2026PAUSEDTRACKBORN

TrackBorn development is paused.

I am putting TrackBorn on hold while I support Mobile MMA Sim and Unwritten Meridian. The project is still part of Poison Games, but I do not want to promise an Early Access timeline while it is not receiving active development time.

02 OCT 2026PAUSEDONLINE MMA SIM

Online MMA Sim remains paused.

The project is not cancelled, but it is not part of the active development workload. I will only bring it back into public updates when development actually resumes.

SEP 2026LESSONPUBLISHING

Public builds changed how I think about “done”.

Testing a game yourself is different from supporting a build after other people can install it. Store tracks, device behaviour, versioning, saves and player reports have made release discipline a much bigger part of how I approach development.

Portfolio vs devlog: the portfolio is the stable overview; this page records dated changes. That separation keeps the site useful instead of repeating the same status paragraph in every section.
