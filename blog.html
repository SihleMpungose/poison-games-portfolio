DEV JOURNEYLESSONS

Why I’m keeping the beginner label.

I want this portfolio to show growth without pretending that every system I build is expert-level work. The useful part of being at this stage is that every project exposes a new gap: architecture, UI, testing, release preparation or simply learning when a feature has become too complicated. The goal is to make the next build cleaner than the last.

More posts will be added as the projects move forward. The landing page stays focused on the portfolio; development notes live here so the two purposes do not compete with each other.
