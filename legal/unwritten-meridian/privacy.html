UNWRITTEN MERIDIAN / BOOK ONE

Privacy Policy

How the current Android and browser editions of Book One handle local saves and player information.

Version 1.1Effective: 30 August 2026Last updated: 5 November 2026
Plain-language summary: The current version of Unwritten Meridian — Book One is an offline game. It does not contain advertising, analytics, user accounts or developer-operated online services. Game choices and saves are stored locally on the player’s device. The developer does not receive or sell that gameplay information.

1. Who is responsible for the Game

Unwritten Meridian — Book One (the “Game”) is published by Sihle Ntokozo Mpungose, South Africa (“the developer”, “we”, “us” or “our”). This Policy explains how information is handled by the current Android and browser versions.

2. Information entered or created during play

The Game allows a player to enter a character name and creates gameplay information such as choices, statistics, relationships, injuries, story progress, save-slot dates and ending results. Players should use a fictional character name and should not enter sensitive personal information.

This information is used only to operate the story, restore saved progress and create a player-requested export file. It is not sent to the developer.

3. Local storage

Save slots and settings are stored locally using storage provided by the player’s browser or Android device. Clearing browser site data, clearing Android app storage or uninstalling the Game may erase locally stored progress.

4. Android system backup

On supported Android devices, the operating system may include local Game data in Android backup or device-to-device transfer. That processing is controlled by the player, platform and backup provider, not the developer.

5. Exported save files

At the end of the Game, a player may choose to export a Book One save file for possible use with Book Two. The player chooses where the file is saved and is responsible for storing, sharing or deleting it. The export is not automatically uploaded to the developer. Anyone who obtains the file may be able to read the character name and gameplay history it contains.

6. Information we collect or transmit

The current Game does not transmit player information to a developer-controlled server. It does not require an account and does not collect contact details, precise location, payment information, photographs, contacts, messages or health information through normal gameplay.

7. Advertising, analytics and third-party SDKs

The current Game does not contain advertisements, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or behavioural tracking. If a future release introduces advertising, analytics or another online service, this Policy and relevant store disclosures will be updated before that release is made available.

8. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell player information. Because the current Game does not send gameplay information to us, we do not share that gameplay information with advertisers, data brokers or analytics providers. A player-directed save export or platform backup is not a sale by the developer.

9. Retention and deletion

Local gameplay information remains on the device until the player overwrites a save, clears app/browser data or uninstalls the Game. Exported files remain wherever the player saved them until deleted. Because we do not receive these local saves, we cannot retrieve or delete them remotely.

10. Security

The Game is designed to minimise privacy risk by keeping current gameplay information on the player’s device. No storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device and avoid sharing exported saves if they contain a real name.

11. Players aged 13 and older

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated account or server.

12. Privacy rights and enquiries

Depending on where a player lives, privacy laws—including South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)—may provide rights concerning personal information. Current gameplay information remains under the player’s local control. Privacy questions can be sent to the contact below.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when features or legal requirements change. Material changes affecting data collection or sharing will be disclosed before the relevant feature is used.
