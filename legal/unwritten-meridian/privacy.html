UNWRITTEN MERIDIAN / BOOK ONE

Privacy Policy

How information is handled by the current Android and browser versions.

Effective: 30 August 2026Last updated: 30 August 2026Hosted on Poison Games: 5 November 2026
Migration note: The substantive policy below is preserved from the existing Unwritten Meridian privacy policy. Hosting has moved to the Poison Games legal hub.
Plain-language summary: The current version of Unwritten Meridian — Book One: The Breach and the Flight is an offline game. It does not contain advertising, analytics, user accounts or developer-operated online services. Game choices and saves are stored locally on the player's device. The developer does not receive or sell that information.

1. Who is responsible for the game

Unwritten Meridian — Book One: The Breach and the Flight (the “Game”) is published by Sihle Mpungose, South Africa (“the developer”, “we”, “us” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled by the current Android and browser versions of the Game.

2. Information entered or created during play

The Game allows a player to enter a character name and creates gameplay information such as choices, statistics, relationships, injuries, story progress, save-slot dates and ending results. Players should use a fictional character name and should not enter sensitive personal information.

This gameplay information is used only to operate the story, restore saved progress and create a player-requested export file. It is not sent to the developer.

3. Local storage

Save slots and settings are stored locally using storage provided by the player's browser or Android device. We do not have access to this local information. Clearing the browser's site data, clearing the Android app's storage or uninstalling the Game may erase locally stored progress.

4. Android system backup

On supported Android devices, the operating system may include local Game data in the player's Android backup or device-to-device transfer. Such backup processing is controlled by the player, the device platform and the backup provider—not by the developer. Players can manage backup settings through their device or Google Account settings.

5. Exported save files

At the end of the Game, a player may choose to export a Book One save file for possible use with Book Two. The player selects where the file is saved and is responsible for storing, sharing or deleting it. The export is not automatically uploaded to the developer. Anyone who obtains the file may be able to read the character name and gameplay history contained in it.

6. Information we collect or transmit

The current Game does not transmit player information to a developer-controlled server. It does not require an account and does not collect contact details, precise location, payment information, photographs, contacts, messages or health information.

7. Advertising, analytics and third-party SDKs

The current Game does not contain advertisements, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or behavioural tracking. If a future release introduces advertising, analytics or another online service, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure will be updated before that release is made available.

8. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell player information. Because the current Game does not send gameplay information to us, we do not share that information with advertisers, data brokers or analytics providers. A player-directed save export or Android platform backup is not a sale by the developer.

9. Retention and deletion

Local gameplay information remains on the player's device until the player overwrites a save, clears the app or browser data, or uninstalls the Game. Exported files remain wherever the player saved them until the player deletes them. Because we do not receive these files or local saves, we cannot retrieve or delete them remotely.

10. Security

The Game is designed to minimise privacy risk by keeping current gameplay information on the player's device. No storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Players should protect their device, use its screen lock, and avoid sharing exported save files if they contain a real name.

11. Players aged 13 and older

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the current Game does not operate a user account or developer server, a parent or guardian can remove local Game information by clearing the Game's storage or uninstalling it.

12. Privacy rights and enquiries

Depending on where a player lives, privacy laws—including South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)—may provide rights concerning personal information. The current Game keeps gameplay information under the player's local control and does not provide it to us. Privacy questions may be submitted using the developer email displayed in the Developer contact section of the Game's official Google Play listing. Please use the subject line “Unwritten Meridian Privacy”.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game's features or legal requirements change. The revised policy will show a new “Last updated” date. Material changes affecting data collection or sharing will be disclosed before the relevant feature is used.
