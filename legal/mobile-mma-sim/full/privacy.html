MOBILE MMA SIM / FULL EDITION

Privacy Policy

How the current paid Full Edition handles gameplay data, local saves, purchases and support information.

Version 1.0Effective: 5 November 2026Last updated: 5 November 2026
Source policy: The existing Mobile MMA Sim Full Edition privacy policy is currently hosted on Google Sites at this official policy page. The Poison Games-hosted version is being used as the long-term replacement; the Google Sites page remains the authoritative legacy source until the migration text has been fully cross-checked.
Privacy at a glance: The current Full Edition does not require a Poison Games account, does not contain advertising or behavioural analytics, and does not send ordinary gameplay saves to a developer-operated server. Career data is stored locally unless you deliberately export or share it.

1. Who is responsible for the Game

Mobile MMA Sim — Full Edition is published by Sihle Ntokozo Mpungose, South Africa, under the developer identity SihleM and the Poison Games brand. This policy applies to the current Full Edition on Android and authorised browser/download platforms.

2. Gameplay information and local saves

The Game creates and stores information needed to run a career, such as fighter details, career decisions, results, rankings, finances, settings and save-state information. Current gameplay data is stored locally on the player’s device or browser environment and is not automatically transmitted to the Developer.

3. Save export, import and backups

Where the Game provides export or import, you choose the file and location. Exported saves may contain player-entered information and career history. Copies you place in cloud storage, messaging apps or other services are handled under those services’ privacy practices.

Device or operating-system backup features may also copy app data according to your device and provider settings. These platform backups are not operated by Poison Games.

4. Accounts, advertising and analytics

The current Full Edition does not require a Poison Games game account and does not include advertising or advertising trackers. The current production release is not described as using developer-operated behavioural analytics. If a future release materially changes these practices, this policy and applicable store disclosures will be updated before or when that change is introduced.

5. Purchases and storefronts

Google Play, itch.io and other authorised storefronts process purchases and account information under their own terms and privacy policies. The Game does not ask you to enter full payment-card details into the game itself.

6. Support and Founder reward verification

If you voluntarily contact support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, screenshots, platform, version and—where relevant to a Founder reward or purchase issue—an order reference, receipt or Supporter Request ID.

This information is used to answer the request, investigate an issue or verify eligibility. Do not send passwords or complete payment-card details, and redact unrelated personal information from receipts.

7. Sharing and sale of information

Poison Games does not sell ordinary gameplay save information. Current gameplay saves are not automatically sent to us for advertising or analytics. Information you voluntarily send for support may be handled by the email or communication service you choose to use.

8. Retention and deletion

Local game progress remains on your device until you delete, overwrite or clear it, subject to any backups you created. Exported save files remain wherever you placed them until you delete them.

Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary to answer or document the request, meet legal obligations or resolve a transaction/support issue. You may request deletion of support correspondence where applicable by contacting us.

9. Security

Keeping ordinary gameplay data local reduces the amount of player data sent to the Developer, but no storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device and exported saves, especially if you use a real name in player-created fields.

10. Children and age ratings

The Game is distributed with age-rating information supplied by the relevant store. The current gameplay does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through a Poison Games account or developer-operated gameplay server.

11. Privacy rights and enquiries

Depending on where you live, privacy laws including South Africa’s POPIA may provide rights concerning personal information. Local-only saves are controlled on your device. For information you voluntarily sent to support, contact us using the email below.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when features, data practices, platforms or legal requirements change. Material changes affecting collection or sharing will be reflected in the policy and relevant platform disclosures.
