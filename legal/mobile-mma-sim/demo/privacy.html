MOBILE MMA SIM / FREE DEMO

Privacy Policy

How the Demo handles local career data and, on Android, optional rewarded advertising.

Version 1.0Effective: 5 November 2026Last updated: 5 November 2026
Privacy at a glance: Career progress is stored locally. The Demo does not require a Poison Games account. The Android Demo may use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to provide optional rewarded advertisements; those third-party services may process information under Google’s own policies.

1. Who is responsible for the Demo

The Mobile MMA Sim Demo is published by Sihle Ntokozo Mpungose, South Africa, under the developer identity SihleM and the Poison Games brand. This policy covers the browser Demo and the separate Android Demo where available.

2. Gameplay information and local saves

The Demo creates information needed to operate your career, such as fighter details, progression, results, settings and save-state information. The Demo is designed to keep ordinary gameplay saves locally in the browser or Android app storage rather than sending them to a Poison Games gameplay server.

3. Demo limit and save files

The current refreshed Demo uses a fixed two-year/96-week career boundary. The Demo may also support player-directed save export/import. Exported files remain under your control and may contain player-entered information and career history.

4. Android rewarded advertising

The Android Demo may display optional rewarded ads using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). If you choose to request or view an ad, Google and its advertising technology may process device, app, network, advertising or diagnostic information needed to request, deliver, measure, secure or limit ads, depending on region, device settings, SDK configuration and consent requirements.

Poison Games does not receive your full payment-card information from this feature and the in-game reward has no cash value. For Google’s current data practices, consult Google’s privacy and advertising documentation.

5. Consent and privacy choices

Where advertising law or platform rules require a consent or privacy choice, the Android Demo should present the applicable options supplied by the configured Google advertising/consent services. Availability of personalised or non-personalised advertising can differ by region and consent state.

6. Browser / itch.io hosting

The browser Demo is hosted through itch.io. itch.io may independently process technical, account, cookie or analytics information when you visit its website under itch.io’s own privacy policy. That platform processing is separate from local career saves created by the Demo.

7. Support information

If you contact Poison Games voluntarily, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, screenshots and device/version details. This is used to answer or investigate the request. Do not send passwords or complete payment-card details.

8. Retention and deletion

Local career data remains until you overwrite it, clear browser/app storage or uninstall the Demo, subject to any device backup or exported copy you created. Delete exported files from the locations where you saved them. Poison Games cannot remotely delete or recover local-only saves that were never sent to us.

9. Data sale and sharing

Poison Games does not sell local career-save information. Third-party advertising or hosting providers may process information independently as described above. Player-initiated export or sharing is controlled by the player.

10. Children and age ratings

The Demo follows the age-rating and audience information shown by the applicable platform. Poison Games does not require a game account for the Demo. Advertising providers may apply additional rules for younger users and regulated regions.

11. Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the Demo’s advertising, platform integrations, save behaviour or legal requirements change. Store data-safety declarations should be kept consistent with the production build and this policy.
