BENEATH THE BORROWED LIGHT

Privacy Policy

How the free itch.io browser release handles local saves and how third-party hosting is separated from the Game itself.

Version 1.0Effective: 5 November 2026Last updated: 5 November 2026
Privacy at a glance: The Game uses browser-local saves and does not require a Poison Games account. Ordinary save data is not automatically sent to Poison Games. itch.io is a separate hosting platform and may process website/account/technical information under its own privacy policy.

1. Who is responsible for the Game

Beneath the Borrowed Light is published by Sihle Ntokozo Mpungose / SihleM under the Poison Games brand. This Policy describes the Game itself; itch.io separately controls its website and account services.

2. Local game data

The Game stores information needed for play, including save-slot progress, checkpoints, settings, accessibility preferences and gameplay state, in browser-local storage associated with the current device/browser and hosting origin.

3. No Poison Games account

The current Game does not require a Poison Games user account and does not ask players to register a profile with us in order to play.

4. Information sent to the Developer

The Game itself is not designed to automatically upload ordinary local save progress to a Poison Games server. If you voluntarily contact us or send feedback, we receive the information you choose to provide.

5. itch.io and third-party hosting

The Game is hosted on itch.io. itch.io may process cookies, device/network information, account activity, payments/donations if you choose to make them, comments or other platform information according to itch.io’s own privacy policy. Poison Games does not control itch.io’s independent platform processing.

6. Advertising and analytics

The current Game does not contain an in-game advertising system operated by Poison Games. The itch.io website may use its own platform analytics, cookies or other services independently from the Game.

7. Retention and deletion

Local save data remains until you clear the relevant browser/site storage or the browser/device removes it. Because ordinary saves are not sent to us, we cannot remotely retrieve, restore or delete them. Any messages you voluntarily send to support may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond or meet legal obligations.

8. Security

No storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect access to your device and browser profile. Avoid sending passwords, banking details or unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.

9. Children and content

The Game contains dark and unsettling themes as disclosed on its itch.io page. Parents and guardians should use the platform’s content information when deciding whether the Game is appropriate. The Game does not require a Poison Games child account.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy if the Game later adds online services, analytics, advertising or different hosting. A new last-updated date will be shown.
